# Dwarf Tossing
A horror game

## Story
_Pre Lore_: As a early-20th-century cave explorer, you dig your way into a buried cave with little equipment. However, as you dare to break the last stones, the your excavated entrance suddendly collapes and buries your pickaxe with it, you manage to save your body by jumping into the cave, but now you are seemingly stuck there.

_Actual Game (spoilers)_: You go on further into the cave, and you quickly make contact with the evil dwarfs. They, try to chase you more and more into the cave, but you may make a quick escape through a long tunnel with a platform at the end, you elevate yourself up and for the next minute, you seem to be in a friendly path, you eat the mushrooms and drink the water near the path and feel saturated. However, now the real story just begins: As you enter a new part of the huge cave, you notice that it gets less and less lively. At this moment, the cave ends, but you notice lights, apparently coming from a stalactite. It looks like a village, you go into the stone structures that look like houses. They are empty. Except for one: "There is a pickaxe!", you shout, and grab it immediately, you go back your path, and mine more of the cobbles that had fallen on your way in. Then, again, the stones fall, the air is now really dusty, you caugh. In the fog, you notice that something fell down: A map. A really old and grubby map. Though, the marking are still clear and there is a very bright highlighted place. As you think to yourself, you get a sudden idea: "It must be a treasure map!". You immediately follow the trail and at the end, you actually find something: Shimmering brightly in the distance, you see crystals. You take the precious green minerals without asking twice. Who should you even? You haven't seen someone in what feels like ages. You go back to the entrance with your new loot. You again, swing our pickaxe. However, now, on the last centimetres you mine, a quiet but still noticable voice nudges you from behind: A blue, friendly looking dwarf is there. As they have your attention, they shly ask straightaway, "Please, leave the crystals in the cave.". You listen carefully, while wondering why you should give them back now. "They are our source of live. Without them, we will die, in not more than a few hours."

_The decision (A)_: You decide to give it back, the dwarf helps you out safely and you live your life happyily, for now at least. 

_The decision (B)_: You decide against giving it back, the dwarf looks at you, cries and the cave collapses, both of you, remain forgotten.
